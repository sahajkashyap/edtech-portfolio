# Audit — Lessons 1–45

**Date:** 31 July 2026
**Scope:** `sheets/lesson-001.html` … `lesson-045.html`, against
`passages/lesson-006.json` … `lesson-045.json` and `sound-list.json`
**Auditor:** independent agent; did not build these sheets; briefed to find
what was wrong rather than confirm it was fine. Report-only — it edited nothing.

---

## 1. Blocking problems

**One real content break of the decodability promise, in two lessons — the
grown-up sheet's printed guarantee is false there.**

- **Lesson 7, story page (p3), line 6:** "As I tap, Sam and Pam pat." The
  curriculum's own data (`sound-list.json` → `heartWordDetail`) says **"as" is
  a heart word taught at Lesson 11** (because the s says /z/). At Lesson 7 a
  child decoding it with the only s-sound they have been taught reads /ass/,
  not /az/. Lesson 7's `allowedHeartWords` are only I, a, and, the — "as" is
  not among them.
- **Lesson 8, story page (p3):** "is" is used repeatedly ("Sam is at a pit. It
  is a mat…"). "is" is the **Lesson 9** heart word, same s = /z/ problem, one
  lesson early.
- In both lessons page 1 prints: *"Checked word by word: all N words in this
  story use only sounds taught by Lesson N, plus heart words already learned.
  Nothing here needs guessing."* For these two sheets that sentence is not true
  — "as"/"is" need exactly the guessing it rules out.
- Why the project's own checker missed it: `audit_passage.py` passes both words
  because a+s and i+s are letter-decodable with s = /s/; it never consults
  `heartWordDetail`'s teaching lessons for words that happen to pass
  letter-by-letter. That is the hole to close.
- Honest severity: a parent will not notice, and a child saying "iss" will
  usually self-correct to the spoken word. But it is the only place in 45
  lessons where the product's core claim is printed and false, which is why I
  rank it here rather than below.

## 2. Should fix

1. **Heart-word preview does not match the story it fronts (systemic, ~26 of 40
   story lessons).** The words page previews the *curriculum's newest* heart
   words, not the story's. In **12 lessons both previewed heart words never
   appear in the story at all**: L16 (to, do), L20 (of, see), L22, L24, L29
   (was, we), L30, L31, L34 (what, have), L35, L37, L42 (your, want), L45 (so,
   goes); in ~14 more, one of the two is absent. Meanwhile the heart words the
   child actually meets in the story ("said", "was", "you", "of", "he"…) get no
   preview. Page 1 explicitly scripts the parent: "Say the practice words… then
   the heart words," then "Read the story" — the flow promises story prep and
   delivers schedule words. Either use story-matched heart words, or relabel
   the block ("This week's heart words") so the parent isn't set up to expect
   them in the story.
2. **Lesson 5 does not follow the agreed lessons-2–5 words-page spec.** Its
   pages are Slide and Read / Sound Boxes / The Changing Word — no fold line,
   no below-fold "Instructions for the grown-up," no read-3-times row. To be
   fair: it is a well-designed blending lesson, all adult text is correctly
   isolated on page 1, and the child pages are clean. But it is a structural
   deviation from what was agreed for 2–5, so the teacher should explicitly
   sign off on it rather than discover it.
3. **Lesson 2 fold-block boilerplate teaches with untaught letters.** The
   below-fold grown-up text on `lesson-002.html` p4 uses "/m/ … /a/ … /t/ does
   not sound like *mat*" — t is not taught until Lesson 4. Adult-facing, so no
   child harm, but the sheet elsewhere is scrupulous about this.

## 3. Cosmetic

- **Lesson 5 page 4 is more than half empty** (one word chain plus two "read it
  and do it" items); Lesson 1 p3 and Lesson 5 p3 also have large blank lower
  halves. Fine for a child, but looks unfinished in a portfolio.
- **Lesson 2 words page:** big awkward vertical hole between the "Touch, then
  slide" heading and the lone word "am"; and the row says "READ THEM 3 TIMES"
  for one word.
- **Stories use straight quotation marks** (L13, L41, L45 visible) while adult
  pages use curly ones. Trivial.
- **Lesson 1 keyword art:** the apple is drawn *as* the letter a with a stem
  and leaf — charming, but a five-year-old shown it cold may just say "a" or "a
  leaf," not "apple." The sort-grid apple (plain circle + stem) could pass for
  an orange. Both are mitigated: the sheet says "Name each picture out loud
  with your grown-up first" and page 1 lists the intended names — that
  instruction is what keeps every picture safe, so keep it forever.
- **Lesson 41 review checklist** (the/to/was/we/what/you) includes three words
  not in Mud Pig and omits "said," which is. Defensible as cumulative review;
  worth a glance.

## 4. Checked and correct

- **Page counts: 45 of 45 correct.** L1 = 3 pages, L2–L5 = 4, L6–L45 = 5; the
  "Prints as N pages" header matches the real `.page` count on every sheet.
- **Overflow: 219 of 219 rendered pages fit** the 940.8px budget
  (`check-pages.py` run on all 45 sheets; worst page ~921px, most under 900).
  Zero overflows.
- **Story on one page: 40 of 40.** Every line of every passage JSON is present
  verbatim, and the complete story sits on exactly page 3 in every story
  packet. No story splits.
- **Dictation: 40 of 40** grown-up sheets carry "The writing sentence · read
  this aloud to them," and every sentence is verbatim from that lesson's story.
- **"All N words" claims: 40 of 40** match the actual story word count.
- **Questions: 40 of 40** have exactly 3 questions, matching the JSON, printed
  on page 5 with answers on page 1, and page 5 is clearly marked
  extra/optional.
- **Fold line: L2, L3, L4 all correct** — fold line present, "Instructions for
  the grown-up" below it, child half above holds only letters, words, and the
  read-3-times row. (L5: see Should fix #2.)
- **Letter hunts L1–L4:** "There are 9 to find" is true in all four grids (24
  cells each), and L2–L4 grids use only taught letters; L1's untaught
  look-alike distractors (o, c, e) are explained to the parent on page 1.
- **Word counts:** L2 = 1 (am — alphabet-limited, expected), L3 = 2 (am, Sam —
  expected), L4 = 5, L5 = 5, L6–L7 = 4, L8–L12 = 5, L13–L45 = 6, plus 2 heart
  words. Nothing thin beyond the expected early lessons.
- **Decodability:** the project's own `audit_passage.py` passes all 40 stories
  at their lesson numbers (with the L7/L8 caveat above, which it structurally
  cannot catch).
- **Visual pass (11 lessons, ~30 pages read as rendered images: 1, 2, 4, 5, 6,
  13, 20, 27, 34, 41, 45):** story type is large with generous leading and the
  single-storey `a` throughout; practice words are big with correct sound-dots
  and sweep arrows (dot counts spot-checked, including 3 for "Sam"); drawing
  box ~5.2in with proper three-line writing rules below; letter formation
  models are correct single-storey forms with numbered strokes and start dots;
  grown-up sheets are genuinely parent-usable (the L20 sheet's answers all
  check out against the story lines they cite); no heavy ink fills, nothing
  runs to the page edge, and nothing depends on colour — the only greys (page
  footers, "YOUR PICTURE" label) are non-essential.

**Bottom line:** structurally this range is in excellent shape — every count a
script can check comes back clean. The two things worth the teacher's attention
are the L7/L8 "as"/"is" early heart words (the one place the printed guarantee
is false, plus the auditor hole that let it through) and the heart-word
preview/story mismatch, which is a design decision currently at odds with the
parent script on page 1.

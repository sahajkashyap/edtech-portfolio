# Audit — Lessons 46–90

**Date:** 31 July 2026
**Scope:** `sheets/lesson-046.html` … `lesson-090.html`
**Auditor:** independent agent; did not build these sheets; briefed to find
what was wrong rather than confirm it was fine. Report-only — it edited no
project files; all probes were throwaway scripts in its scratchpad.

---

## 1. Blocking problems

**L85, story page (p3): a story line wraps.** "Ana swam in the green sea with
her mom." renders with "mom." alone on a second line — screenshot-verified.
This is the one hard visual rule ("the line is the unit a child points along")
and it is broken.

Worse, the repo's checker can never catch this: `wrap_check.py` counts
`getClientRects()` on `.passage .ln`, but `.ln` is `display: block`, and a
block element returns **one** rect even when its text wraps. Its "0 sheets
wrap" result is a false clean for the whole project — the 1–45 and 91–128
ranges should be re-swept with a Range-based probe. In 46–90 this was the only
wrap.

**L46 ("Mud, Then the Tub", Voiced th): the word "both" teaches the wrong sound
at the exact lesson teaching the contrast.** "both" is warm-up word #1 and
appears three times in the story. Its th is *unvoiced* /θ/ (taught at Lesson
47), and its long o before th is untaught at 46 — the project's own
`sound-list.json` introduces "both" as a **heart word at Lesson 72** (heart on
the o). Yet `word-bank.json` has `newAtLesson["46"]: ["both", ...]`, so the
auditor passes it. The grown-up sheet's green audit box even asserts "every
word here uses only the one [th sound] taught so far" — false. A parent
following the sheet will coach the child to sound out /b-ŏ-ð/ and get a
non-word.

The same bank entry has other oddities ("withs"; "moths/baths/paths" available
one lesson before their singulars) — worth a bank re-check, but "both" at 46 is
the only one that reached a printed sheet in my range.

## 2. Should fix

- **All 45 grown-up sheets, stale step wording.** Step 1: "Say the practice
  words **at the top of the story page**" — since the five-page redesign the
  words are on their own page (p2); the top of the story page has nothing.
  Step 4: "They write one sentence **on the lines under the story**" — the
  lines are on p4, under the drawing box. A parent with no teaching background
  (the stated audience) is sent to the wrong page twice.
- **All 45: step order contradicts the page.** Steps number writing (4) before
  drawing (5), but page 4 puts drawing above writing, and DECISIONS.md records
  the teacher's order as "read it, draw it, then write." A literal parent does
  the packet backwards from the page's own layout.
- **Heart cards rarely belong to the story.** In 44 of 45 lessons at least one
  heart card (usually both) is a word that never appears in that story — e.g.
  L90 teaches "floor" and "son"; the story uses neither. The cards are clearly
  the *newest* heart words in the scope, which is defensible, but grown-up step
  1 frames them as prep for this story. Either the framing or the selection
  should change.

## 3. Cosmetic

- Every sheet has one stray extra `</div>` before `</body>` (55 open / 56
  close; L79 is 53/54). Browsers ignore it; no visible effect.
- L74 continuity: the puppy digs the penny "in the mud" by the pond, but the
  lady "lost a penny in the grass."
- Several story pages (46, 59) leave the bottom ~25% blank despite "type grows
  to fill the page" — the no-wrap width ceiling is binding, so this is
  explained, but they read airier than the promise.

## 4. Checked and correct

- **Pages:** 45/45 have exactly 5 `.page` divs; header "Prints as 5 pages"
  matches in all.
- **Story:** 45/45 have the story on exactly one page (p3); every line of every
  `passages/lesson-NNN.json` present verbatim, in order; titles match.
- **Words page:** 45/45 warm-up words match JSON and sit with the heart cards
  on p2; nothing crammed onto the story page.
- **Page 4:** 45/45 have the drawing box above two three-line handwriting
  groups (solid/dotted/solid confirmed on-screen; rules 0.62in tall, box
  ~5.2in).
- **Questions:** 45/45 p5 marked "This page is extra"; questions match JSON;
  grown-up answer count matches; spot-checked line-number references in answers
  (46, 85) are correct.
- **Dictation:** 45/45 "say" sentences are genuine story lines. Word-count
  claims ("all N words") accurate in 45/45.
- **Overflow:** all 225 pages fit the 940.8px budget via `check-pages.py`;
  tightest is L67 p1 at 921px.
- **Type size:** per-lesson story sizes range 22–29px (min 22px at L59) — all
  above the 18px floor.
- **Visual:** 12 sheets sampled across all five page types (46, 51, 55, 59, 63,
  67, 68, 72, 77, 82, 85, 86, 90): single-storey "a" confirmed, black-on-white
  and printer-safe, no heavy ink fills, nothing at page edges, greys legible,
  page 1 genuinely usable by a lay parent.
- **Stories as a teacher:** read 15+ in full — coherent cause-and-effect, the
  child acts while adults assist (Dad holds the lamp, the zoo man hands the
  tool, the child does the solving), nothing frightening, sad, or medical. All
  45 pass `validate_passage.py` and `audit_passage.py` — with the L46 "both"
  caveat above, which the tooling missed because of the bank tag, not the
  checker logic.

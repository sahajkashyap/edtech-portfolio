Lessons 1–5 have no passage, on purpose. No decodable passage exists this early. A story needs a vocabulary, and these lessons are still introducing single letter-sounds — at Lesson 1 there are no words a child can sound out at all, and by Lesson 5 there are five (am, as, at, mat, sat). Letter-sound practice for these lessons comes from the worksheet generator in the assessment tool instead.

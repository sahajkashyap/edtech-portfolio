A story needs a vocabulary, and these lessons are still introducing single letter-sounds — at Lesson 1 there are no words a child can sound out at all, and by Lesson 5 there are five (am, as, at, mat, sat). Letter-sound practice for these lessons comes from the worksheet generator in the assessment tool instead. So those five teach the letter and its sound instead: the letter with a keyword picture, how the mouth makes the sound, handwriting practice on three-line guides, a letter hunt and a beginning-sound sort — and, at Lesson 5, first blending.

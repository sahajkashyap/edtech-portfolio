Before you start · is your child ready for this one?

This is Lesson 20. It assumes your child has already worked through Lessons 1–19. Each lesson only uses sounds taught in the ones before it. If your child has not had those yet, this story will be too hard — not because of anything they did, but because the sounds have not been introduced yet. Ask their teacher which sheet to start with.

About 5 minutes, in this order

1. Look at the picture together. Ask “What do you see? What do you think happens?” This puts the story in their head before their eyes do the hard work.

2. Cover the picture while they read. It is there for understanding, not for guessing words. Uncover it when they finish.

3. Warm up on the words, check off the heart words, then read the story.

4. If they get stuck, don’t say the word. Ask them to sound it out or use their sound spelling — the words their teacher uses. Tap under each letter as they go.

5. Read it three times, then draw what happened. The drawing is the comprehension check at this age — no writing needed, and the picture shows you what they understood.

The last sheet of questions is extra. Reading and drawing is the work — stopping there is finishing.

If they want the questions

Optional. They work just as well asked out loud over the drawing.

1. How many cups did Peg set on the mat?
Ten. It is in the first line. If your child is not sure, put a finger on that line and let them read it again.

2. Which cup had the nut in it?
The tin cup. Line six says it. If they say "the last one," ask them to find the line that names it.

3. What did Ted tap first? What did he tap after that?
The big cup, then the tan cup, then the tin cup. Lines four to six hold the order.

Why you can trust this page · checked, word by word

Every one of the 62 words in this story can be sounded out with what your child has already been taught. Nothing here needs guessing. If they get stuck, the answer is always “sound it out” or “use your sound spelling” — never “let me tell you.”

The only words not sounded out are the 11 heart words taught so far — and even those are mostly regular. Only the marked part of each is learned by heart. The newest are is of said see the to.

Ten Cups and a Nut

Peg sets ten cups on a mat. Peg tips a nut in a cup. Peg taps the cups. "Get it, Ted!" Ted taps a big cup. Not in it. Ted taps a tan cup. Not it! Ted taps the tin cup and gets the nut! Peg nods. Ted gets to tip the nut in. Peg taps the cups, and Peg gets it!

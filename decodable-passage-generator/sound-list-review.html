The rulebook behind every decodable passage. For each of the 128 lessons it records exactly which letters, letter teams and heart words a reader has been taught by that point — so a story can be checked against it word by word instead of by eye.

Lessons are cumulative. A child on Lesson 41 has had 1 through 40. UFLI publishes no other prerequisites — the sequence is simply in order, which is what every sheet’s opening banner says.

17 lessons corrected against the UFLI manual

Checked against UFLI Foundations' published Toolbox and Scope & Sequence. Your lesson order is right — every lesson number teaches what UFLI says it teaches, in the right sequence. These 17 entries had typos in the wording; the corrections are applied here. The same typos are still in your assessment tool and will come back unless they are fixed there too.

Lesson 10
CVC Practice (g, i) → CVC Practice (a, i)

tool says '(g, i)'; UFLI has (a, i). The letter g is Lesson 16.

Lesson 11
Nasalized A (am, on) → Nasalized A (am, an)

tool says '(am, on)'; UFLI has am/an.

Lesson 27
l /l/ Part 2, ai → l /l/ Part 2

tool says 'l /l/ Part 2, ai'. UFLI lists no second item; the stray letters are the -al ending misread as 'ai'. The vowel team ai is Lesson 84, and is NOT taught here.

Lesson 51
ng /n/ → ng /ng/

notation only; tool wrote /n/.

Lesson 52
nk /nk/ → nk /ngk/

notation only; tool wrote /nk/.

Lesson 58
u /ū/, /ū/ → u_e /u/, /yu/

tool says 'u /u/, /u/'. It is the VCe pattern u_e, and it carries a second sound /yu/ (cube) the tool lost.

Lesson 67
Closed/Closed → Compound Words, Closed/Closed

tool says 'Closed/Closed'. UFLI splits this into 67a Compound Words and 67b Closed/Closed; compound words were missing.

Lesson 90
oo /ŭ/ → oo /oo/ (moon)

tool wrote a breve, which is the book sound. UFLI Lesson 90 is the long oo. Lesson 89 is the other one.

Lesson 93
au, aw, ugh /aw/ → au, aw, augh /aw/

tool says 'ugh'; UFLI has augh (caught, taught).

Lesson 94
ə /ə/, ə /ə/ → ea /e/ (head), a /o/ (want)

tool says 'schwa'. UFLI has no schwa lesson. Lesson 94 teaches short e spelled ea, and short o spelled a. This was the single largest error: it omitted two real sound-spellings and admitted one UFLI does not teach.

Lesson 98
Silent Letters (kn, wr, mb, m) → Silent Letters (kn, wr, mb)

tool says '(kn, wr, mb, m)'. UFLI teaches THREE patterns here. The stray 'm' is the /m/ of 'mb /m/' split off in typing. It is not 'mn' -- mn is not taught anywhere in the 128.

Lesson 111
-ar, -or, -er/ → ar /er/, or /er/

tool says '-ar, -or, -er/'; the third item is spurious.

Lesson 113
ear /ɛr/ → ear /ear/ (hear)

tool wrote the /er/ sound. UFLI Lesson 113 is the 'hear' sound, not the 'her' sound.

Lesson 114
Alternate /ā/ (ei, ey, eigh, high, ea) → Alternate /a/ (ei, eigh, ey, ea, aigh)

tool says 'high'; UFLI has 'aigh'. Previously dropped, so aigh was never taught at all.

Lesson 116
ough /aw/, /ə/ → ough /aw/, /o/

tool wrote schwa for the second sound; UFLI has long o.

Lesson 117
Signal Vowels (c /s/, s /z/, g /j/) → Signal Vowels (c /s/, g /j/)

tool adds 's /z/'; UFLI's signal-vowel lesson is c and g only.

Lesson 127
uni-, bi-, tri → bi-, tri-, uni-

tool drops the hyphen on 'tri'.

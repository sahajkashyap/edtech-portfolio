What's happening

You mark. This counts. Every number here is arithmetic on what you marked — accuracy, error and self‑correction rates, rate per minute, and the M/S/V tally. Nothing here decides how well the child is reading. That judgement is yours; this just puts every piece of it in one place, fast enough to do often.

Notes

Retell
Print gives you one page for the learning specialist: the marked passage, every miscue listed, the numbers and your notes. Export writes a spreadsheet of every saved record. Clear all marks keeps this child and starts the read over; Start a new record moves on to the next child and leaves this one saved.
Repetitions and self-corrections are not counted as errors. Words correct per minute is measured over the whole passage, not a one-minute timing, so it is not comparable to a DIBELS benchmark score. Kept on the teacher's laptop; not stored or sent anywhere.
